Node-Kayles

Click a vertex to remove it and its neighbors. The player left without a move loses. Toggle the margin notes below to see each move's resulting nimber, computed live.

Computation runs client-side in JavaScript, porting the same memoized-recursion algorithm (component decomposition + symmetry canonicalization) used in the underlying research solver, written originally in C++. Board lengths above roughly 12–14 (width 3–4) or 8–10 (width 5–6) may compute more slowly, since nimber calculation is exponential in the worst case. Widths 1, 2, 3, 4, and 6 are checked against known published values; width 5 uses the same validated algorithm but has no published reference sequence to check against.
